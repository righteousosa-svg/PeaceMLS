Overview

Capacity when you need it, managed end to end.

When your freight needs to move and your own trucks or usual carrier can’t cover it, Peacemaker’s brokerage desk sources capacity across our network of vetted carriers — flatbed, hotshot, and beyond — and manages the shipment from pickup through delivery. We broker all phases of freight, including:

  • Oversize & Heavy Haul Freight
  • FTL (Full Truck Load)
  • PTL (Partial Truck Load)
  • LTL (Less-Than Truck Load)
  • Expedited Freight
What’s Included
  • Carrier vetting — insurance & authority verified
  • Rate quoting & scheduling
  • Load tracking & status updates
  • A single point of contact, start to finish
  • Proof of delivery & documentation
